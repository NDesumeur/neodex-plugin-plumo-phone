# Plumo

A one-button skill game: fly a little bird between stone columns. It falls on its own, and each wing flap lifts it up. Get past as many columns as you can and earn medals.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Phone version

You play with one finger: each tap on the screen flaps the wings. The NEW GAME, PAUSE and FULL SCREEN buttons sit right under the game, and the game pauses by itself when you leave the app (a call, the screen turning off). The game fills the phone's width; records, medals, birds and worlds are below.

## Worlds, birds and medals (new in 1.2.0)

- Four **worlds**, each with its own record: **Meadow**; **Forest** (fireflies between the trunks, +1 point each); **City** (buildings move up and down from 10 points); **Snow** (the wind blows from the start, snow falls). Each has a day and a night sky.
- Each **bird** flies its own way: Plumo balanced, **Robin** light (falls gently, the wind pushes it), **Tit** small (gets through more easily), **Flamingo** heavy (big flaps, the wind barely pushes it), **Phoenix** comes back to life once per game.
- A **medal is announced in game** as soon as a milestone is passed: bronze (10), silver (25), gold (50), platinum (100) and the new **diamond** (150).

## Controls

- **Click**, **Space**, **↑** or **Enter**: one wing flap, giving an upward push.
- **P**: pause; a click resumes.
- After a fall, a click starts a new game.

## Rules

- The bird keeps falling: flap at the right rhythm to get through the gap in each column.
- Touching a column or the ground ends the game.
- Each column passed is worth 1 point.
- The gaps narrow little by little (by about a third after forty or so points) and the bird goes faster and faster, more than half as fast again at 60 points.

## The sky

The scenery changes every 25 points: day, evening, starry night, then dawn, and the cycle starts over.

## Medals

At the end of each game, a medal based on your score: **bronze** at 10 points, **silver** at 25, **gold** at 50, **platinum** at 100. The medals panel counts how many of each kind you have earned.

## After 50 points

- Some **columns move** up and down (two small white marks show them).
- **Gusts of wind** blow from time to time: "GUST" shows in yellow one second before, then the wind pushes the bird up (↑) or down (↓) for two to three seconds.

## Birds

Each medal won at least once unlocks a bird: **Robin** (bronze), **Blue tit** (silver), **Flamingo** (gold) and **Phoenix** (platinum, with its trail of fire). Pick it in the **Birds** box; **Plumo** stays available.

## Gamepad and full screen

With a gamepad: **A**, **B**, the d-pad or the stick up to flap, **Start** to pause. The **Full screen** button fills the whole screen with the sky (Escape to leave); otherwise the game takes the full height of the window.

## Statistics

Best score, number of games and total wing flaps.

## Data and sync

Your record, medals and statistics are stored by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs when sync is on.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → Plumo → Install**.
